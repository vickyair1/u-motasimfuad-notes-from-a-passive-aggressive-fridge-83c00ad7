One funny passive-aggressive note that a smart fridge leaves for its owner — a single line, dry, petty and specific, the sort of thing a smug appliance would put on its door screen.

## The note

> You opened me 14 times today and took nothing. I am not a window. Pick something or close me.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) deterministically writes the note to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, then prints the character count (93, within the 1–200 gate).
